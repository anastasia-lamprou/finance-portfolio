03 / KEY FINDINGS

Results that deserve a closer look.

The findings identify areas for investigation; they do not establish the underlying causes of differences in performance.

Differences in profitability across countries

Germany records a gross margin of 15.66% and France 15.53%, both above the overall average of 14.23%. The United States records 11.97%, or 2.26 percentage points below the average. Reviewing US sales prices, product mix and costs could help explain the difference.

Lower margins in higher-discount transactions

Transactions with high discounts have a gross margin of 9.07%, compared with 21.86% for transactions without discounts. The difference is 12.79 percentage points. This suggests a need to assess whether higher-discount sales generate enough additional volume and gross profit to justify their lower margin. The data alone do not prove that discounts caused the entire difference.

Profit contribution and concentration

Paseo generates approximately $4.80m of gross profit, the largest product contribution. The Government customer segment accounts for approximately 67.4% of total gross profit, while Enterprise records a gross loss of approximately $615k. These results warrant separate reviews of concentration and loss-making activity.

04 / METHODOLOGY

From source data to financial insights.

A structured Excel-based process for converting transaction-level data into comparable financial indicators and business conclusions.

01 / Data preparation

Classification of records by product, country, customer segment and discount category.

02 / Financial aggregation

Consolidation of sales and gross profit using Excel PivotTables.

03 / Margin calculation

Calculation of weighted gross margins from aggregated values, rather than averages of individual transaction percentages.

Gross margin = Total gross profit ÷ Total net sales
04 / Benchmark comparison

Comparison of category margins with the overall 14.23% gross margin; calculation of deviations in percentage points (pp).

Margin difference (pp) = Category margin − Overall margin
05 / Visualization

Development of profitability comparisons, charts and executive KPIs.

06 / Business interpretation

Identification of performance gaps, analytical limitations and potential management priorities.
